# Book Club

The family book club: one club reads one current book, keeps a history book for it, and talks about it in comments.

## Language

**Current book**:
The book the club is reading now.
_Avoid_: active book

**History book**:
The club's stored record of a book.
_Avoid_: archive entry

**Past book**:
A history book that is not the current book. The screen is labeled Past books.
_Avoid_: Already read. That phrase is the status shown when a listed book cannot be chosen.

**Comment**:
A member's written remark on a history book.
_Avoid_: note, personal note

**Next book**:
The book the owner is choosing to follow the current book. The club home card for that choice is titled Next book.
_Avoid_: conclude meeting, conclude book

**Shortlist**:
The books the club can still choose as the next book.
_Avoid_: queue, backlog

**Next meeting**:
The one upcoming date and time the owner has set for the club to gather. It is shown in Club information.
_Avoid_: Present meeting, repeating schedule

**Present meeting**:
The in-app screen the club uses while a round is being presented.
_Avoid_: next meeting

**Club genre preferences**:
The genres members have chosen for this round, counted by how many members chose each one.
_Avoid_: favorite genres
